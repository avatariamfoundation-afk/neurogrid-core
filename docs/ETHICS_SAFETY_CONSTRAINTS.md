# ETHICS_SAFETY_CONSTRAINTS.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **ethical guardrails, safety constraints, and non-negotiable principles** governing the design, deployment, and evolution of NeuroGrid.

It exists to:
- Protect against misuse
- Prevent ethical overreach
- Provide judges and auditors with clear assurances
- Establish long-term system integrity

NeuroGrid is intentionally designed to **limit its own power**.

---

## 2. Foundational Ethical Position

NeuroGrid adheres to the following core ethical stance:

> **Infrastructure must never replace human judgment, medical authority, or scientific accountability.**

The system prioritizes:
- Determinism over autonomy  
- Traceability over intelligence  
- Accountability over optimization  

---

## 3. Explicit Safety Constraints (Hard Limits)

NeuroGrid enforces the following **hard constraints by design**:

### 3.1 No Autonomous Decision-Making
- No self-directed execution
- No self-initiated actions
- No autonomous escalation paths

All actions require:
- Explicit invocation
- Deterministic execution
- Logged provenance

---

### 3.2 No Clinical Authority
NeuroGrid cannot:
- Diagnose
- Treat
- Recommend
- Interpret medical meaning

Any attempt to extend NeuroGrid into these domains violates system intent.

---

### 3.3 No Patient Interaction
NeuroGrid:
- Does not interface with patients
- Does not ingest patient-facing data
- Does not provide outputs to end-users

It operates strictly **behind the boundary** of user-facing systems.

---

## 4. Ethical Design Principles

### 4.1 Determinism Over Intelligence
The system favors:
- Predictable outcomes
- Repeatable execution
- Verifiable state transitions

Over:
- Probabilistic reasoning
- Heuristic behavior
- Emergent intelligence

---

### 4.2 Transparency Over Performance
All critical actions must be:
- Observable
- Traceable
- Auditable

Hidden optimizations are discouraged.

---

### 4.3 Accountability Over Scale
NeuroGrid scales only where:
- Accountability is preserved
- Provenance remains intact
- Governance is enforceable

---

## 5. Governance Safeguards

Ethical compliance is enforced through:

| Mechanism | Purpose |
|--------|--------|
| Deterministic governance | Prevents arbitrary control |
| On-chain proposals | Ensures visibility |
| Role separation | Limits power concentration |
| Upgrade delays | Allows review and challenge |

No single actor can unilaterally alter system behavior.

---

## 6. Misuse Prevention

The following uses are **explicitly disallowed**:

- Surveillance of individuals
- Behavioral manipulation
- Social scoring systems
- Covert data aggregation
- Undisclosed experimentation
- Regulatory evasion

Any integration enabling these outcomes violates project ethics.

---

## 7. Human-in-the-Loop Mandate

All deployments must maintain:
- Human oversight
- Human accountability
- Human authority

NeuroGrid **cannot** be the final arbiter of outcomes.

---

## 8. Safety-by-Design Architecture

Safety is enforced through:
- Deterministic fault codes
- Explicit failure modes
- Slashing and accountability telemetry
- No hidden execution paths
- No silent state mutation

Failures are designed to be **loud**, **visible**, and **actionable**.

---

## 9. Alignment With Global Ethical Standards

While not bound to specific medical boards, NeuroGrid aligns conceptually with:

- WHO principles on digital health
- OECD AI accountability guidelines
- IEEE ethical system design principles
- Open science and reproducibility norms

Alignment is **conceptual**, not regulatory.

---

## 10. Ethical Evolution Constraint

Future changes must:
- Preserve existing safeguards
- Not expand autonomous authority
- Not blur medical boundaries
- Be publicly auditable

Ethics are **not optional upgrades**.

---

## 11. Judge & Auditor Assurance

This document exists to demonstrate:

- Intentional ethical restraint
- Long-term responsibility
- Zero tolerance for ambiguity
- System maturity beyond prototype hype

---

## 12. Final Statement

NeuroGrid is designed to be:

- Technically powerful
- Ethically constrained
- Operationally boring
- Structurally honest

That combination is intentional.
