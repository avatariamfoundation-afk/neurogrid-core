# SAFETY & BOUNDARY VALIDATION  
**NeuroGrid-Core**  
**Document ID:** SAFETY-BOUNDARY_VALIDATION.md  
**Status:** Final — Judge-Ready  
**Scope:** Hackathon Evaluation / Research Demonstration  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-10  

---

## 1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT

This document formally defines the **safety boundaries, operational limits, and non-clinical scope** of the NeuroGrid-Core system.

Its purpose is to:
- Prevent misinterpretation of system capabilities  
- Explicitly prohibit unsafe or regulated use  
- Demonstrate responsible AI and medical-software design  
- Provide judges with a clear risk-containment framework  

This document is **foundational**, not aspirational.

---

## 2. SYSTEM CLASSIFICATION

**NeuroGrid-Core is classified as:**

- A **research-grade computational neuroscience platform**
- A **synthetic-data-driven demonstration system**
- A **non-clinical, non-diagnostic, non-therapeutic tool**

NeuroGrid-Core is **NOT**:
- A medical device  
- A clinical decision support system  
- A diagnostic engine  
- A treatment or monitoring platform  

---

## 3. EXPLICIT NON-CLINICAL BOUNDARIES

NeuroGrid-Core **DOES NOT** perform or claim to perform any of the following:

- ❌ Medical diagnosis  
- ❌ Disease prediction  
- ❌ Patient risk scoring  
- ❌ Treatment recommendations  
- ❌ Clinical triage  
- ❌ Prognostic assessment  
- ❌ Medication guidance  
- ❌ Post-operative decision support  

No output generated by NeuroGrid-Core may be interpreted as medical advice.

---

## 4. DATA SAFETY BOUNDARIES

### 4.1 Synthetic Data Only

All data used within NeuroGrid-Core is:

- Algorithmically generated  
- Statistically simulated  
- Non-identifiable  
- Non-patient-specific  

There is:
- No ingestion of real patient data  
- No linkage to clinical datasets  
- No training on proprietary medical records  

### 4.2 Re-Identification Risk

Re-identification risk is **structurally zero** because:
- No real individuals exist in the dataset
- No demographic mapping is preserved
- No external datasets are referenced or joined

---

## 5. AI OUTPUT SAFETY CONSTRAINTS

NeuroGrid-Core enforces **intentional output limitations**:

- Outputs are **descriptive**, not prescriptive  
- Pattern insights are **population-agnostic**  
- No probability-based medical conclusions are produced  
- Confidence thresholds are capped to prevent over-interpretation  

The system does not generate:
- Clinical likelihoods  
- Diagnostic probabilities  
- Actionable health instructions  

---

## 6. USER INTERACTION BOUNDARIES

NeuroGrid-Core is designed so that:

- Users cannot input real patient identifiers  
- Users cannot request diagnosis or treatment  
- Unsafe queries are ignored or rejected  
- Outputs are framed as **research signals only**

Any attempt to use the system beyond defined scope violates intended use.

---

## 7. DEMO-SPECIFIC SAFETY ENFORCEMENT

During demonstrations:

- Only synthetic datasets are executed  
- Output warnings are visible by design  
- Clinical interpretations are explicitly suppressed  
- Demo artifacts halt if unsafe inputs are detected  

This ensures safe public presentation.

---

## 8. REGULATORY POSITIONING (NON-CLAIM)

NeuroGrid-Core is intentionally positioned to fall **outside** regulated medical software definitions by:

- Avoiding patient-specific outputs  
- Avoiding diagnostic intent  
- Avoiding treatment relevance  

This aligns with:
- Early-stage research software norms  
- Non-device exploratory AI frameworks  
- Academic neuroscience simulation standards  

No regulatory approval is claimed or implied.

---

## 9. FUTURE TRANSITION DISCLAIMER

Any future evolution toward clinical, diagnostic, or therapeutic use would require:

- Formal regulatory review  
- Independent medical AI audit  
- Clinical validation trials  
- Ethics board approval  
- Jurisdiction-specific compliance  

**None of these steps are represented in the current system.**

---

## 10. FINAL SAFETY ASSERTION

NeuroGrid-Core has been intentionally engineered to prioritize:

- Human safety  
- Clinical non-interference  
- Responsible AI boundaries  
- Research-only integrity  

This system is safe **because of what it refuses to do**.
